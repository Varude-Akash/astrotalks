Delete your AstroTalks account

You can permanently delete your account and associated data either inside the app or by emailing our support team.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open AstroTalks and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Tap Delete profile.
  4. Confirm Delete permanently.

This is the quickest option.

Request deletion without the app

Email us from the Google email address connected to your AstroTalks account. Use the prepared link below:

Request account deletion

We may ask you to verify account ownership. We aim to complete verified requests within 7 days.

Data that will be deleted

Your authentication account, profile and birth details, conversation sessions and messages, points ledger and app-linked transaction records will be deleted. Ended chats are otherwise retained for no more than 30 days.

Possible limited retention

We may retain minimal purchase, security, fraud-prevention or legal records only where required by law or reasonably necessary to establish or defend legal claims. Such retained information is not used to continue providing an AstroTalks profile.
