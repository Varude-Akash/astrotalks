Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 1 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AstroTalks ("AstroTalks", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the AstroTalks Android application. AstroTalks provides AI-generated spiritual reflection conversations. These experiences are not actual communication with a deity and do not guarantee blessings, miracles, wealth or supernatural outcomes.

Information we collect

  • Account information: Google account email address, Google display name and a Supabase user identifier used for sign-in and account continuity.
  • Profile information: first name, selected religion, date of birth, birth time and birthplace.
  • Conversation information: messages you submit, AI responses, selected spiritual guide, session status, timestamps and billable session duration.
  • Points and transaction information: points balance, usage history and, when purchases are enabled, product and transaction records. Payment credentials are processed by Google Play and are not stored by AstroTalks.
  • Technical information: limited network request information necessary to authenticate, deliver the service, prevent abuse and diagnose failures. AstroTalks does not request device GPS, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or advertising ID access.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, save profiles across devices, personalise spiritual reflections, deliver and archive conversations, operate timers and points, verify purchases, provide support, secure the service and comply with law. Birth details are stored for personalisation but are not used to make guaranteed predictions or consequential decisions.

Service providers and data transfers

We use service providers only as needed to operate AstroTalks:

  • Google: Google Sign-In and, when enabled, Google Play Billing.
  • Supabase: authentication, hosted database and server-side functions.
  • OpenAI: processes the conversation context required to generate an AI response. Do not submit highly sensitive information that is unnecessary for your reflection.
  • Geoapify: receives birthplace search text to return place suggestions. AstroTalks does not request your device's live location.
  • RevenueCat: when purchases are enabled, processes purchase entitlement and transaction identifiers; it does not receive payment-card credentials from AstroTalks.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours and apply their own security and privacy terms. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Retention

Ended conversation content is retained for up to 30 days and then scheduled for deletion. Active conversation content is retained until the conversation ends. Profile, account, points and transaction records are kept while your account remains active. Some minimal purchase, fraud-prevention or legal records may be retained where required by law or necessary to establish or defend legal claims.

Security

Data is transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections. Database access is restricted by authenticated user access controls. No system is completely secure, but we use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the service.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit profile information, sign out, and permanently delete your account from Profile & privacy → Delete profile in the app. You can also use our external account deletion page. Deletion removes the AstroTalks authentication account and associated profile, conversations and points records, subject only to legally required retention described above.

Children

AstroTalks is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information.

Changes

We may update this policy when the product or applicable requirements change. The current version and effective date will always be posted here.
